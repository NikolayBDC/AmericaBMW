Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026. This policy explains what information is submitted when you contact Bavarian Garage 92 through the website, why it is needed, and how you can manage your data.

1. Who receives your request

The data from the request is received by the site owner and specialist operating under the Bavarian Garage 92 brand. For privacy questions, contact via Telegram @NikolayBDC or WhatsApp. Service is provided remotely only across the USA and Canada.

2. What data is processed

The request form may transmit:

  • name;
  • phone number (optional);
  • BMW model and year, if you choose to provide them;
  • vehicle VIN, if you choose to provide it;
  • selected service and comment about the task.

The VIN is needed to check configuration and compatibility of a specific feature. Do not send documents, payment details, or other information not required for discussing the service.

3. Why the data is used

  • to contact you regarding your request;
  • to clarify the vehicle, task, and suitable remote work format;
  • to pre-check service compatibility by VIN;
  • to agree on booking, scope, and terms;
  • to answer related questions.

Data will not be used for unrelated marketing without separate consent.

4. How consent is given

You provide data voluntarily by filling out the form and clicking the submit button. By sending a request, you consent to the processing of the provided information as necessary to respond and organize the service. Consent can be withdrawn via the contacts in Section 1.

5. Third-party processor Formspree

The form on this site sends entered values through Formspree, an external form delivery service. Therefore, data is transmitted to Formspree and may be processed by its infrastructure according to that service's rules. See the Formspree Privacy Policy.

If you choose to communicate via WhatsApp or Telegram, you independently select that channel, and further message processing depends on their rules. External site services, including hosting, fonts, and maps, may receive technical request information such as IP address and browser data according to their own policies.

6. Sharing and access

Data may be accessible to the site owner, to Formspree as the form delivery service, and to your chosen communication channel. Data may also be disclosed to competent authorities if required by applicable mandatory laws. No sale or sharing for third-party advertising purposes is intended.

7. Retention period

Data is kept as long as needed to process the request, perform the agreed service, and resolve directly related matters. After that need ends, data is deleted or anonymized unless further retention is required by applicable laws. No single fixed retention period applies to all requests.

8. Security and minimization

Reasonable measures are used to limit access to requests and transmit only necessary information. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can guarantee absolute security. You can reduce risk by not providing unnecessary information.

9. Your rights

You may contact us via the contact in Section 1 and request to:

  • learn what data from your request is being processed;
  • correct or update information;
  • stop further contact regarding the request;
  • withdraw consent and delete data when storage is no longer required;
  • restrict processing as provided by applicable rules.

To protect against deletion by someone else, we may require reasonable verification that the request comes from you.

10. Policy changes

This policy may be updated if the form, communication methods, or connected services change. The current version and its update date are published on this page.
