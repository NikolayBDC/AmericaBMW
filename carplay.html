Compatibility

First we determine
your system remotely

Year of manufacture alone does not give an exact answer. The head unit, software version, market, and installed hardware matter. All checks are done via your laptop and ENET cable.

For compatible NBT EVO and newer systems I check the available activation method, license status, and required hardware. Screen format and wireless connection depend on the specific version.

If the OEM antenna is missing, I guide you through its installation. After installation, we test connection stability and reconnection remotely.

OEM Android Auto is not available on all head units: as a rule, a newer-generation multimedia system is required. For NBT EVO and EntryNav, Android Auto cannot be promised as a simple software activation — a separate remote check is needed first.
