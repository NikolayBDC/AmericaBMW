From symptom
to cause

I don't just read fault codes: I compare them with current parameters and the configuration of the specific vehicle — all through your ENET connection.

I check the engine, transmission, chassis, safety systems, body electronics, climate control, and multimedia. I separate current faults, stored events, and consequences of the main problem.

I compare available values in different modules and check engine hours. This is especially useful before buying a used car.

I check sensor readings and, when supported by the system, run tests of pumps, fans, flaps, and other actuators via remote commands.

I explain the findings in plain language: what needs attention now, what can be monitored, and what does not affect operation. On request, I save the diagnostic results.

!
Before buying a BMW

Remote electronic diagnostics complement a body and mechanical inspection, but do not replace them. For a full vehicle check, it is best to combine all three stages.
