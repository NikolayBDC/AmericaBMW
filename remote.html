How it works

Secure remote
sessions

Every step is designed to keep your vehicle safe and the process transparent, even from a distance.

We confirm VIN, task, and compatibility. You set up the ENET cable, ensure stable power for car and laptop, and install the remote access tool we agreed on.

I connect to your laptop, read the vehicle configuration, and save a full backup before any changes. You watch everything on screen.

I perform only the agreed coding or diagnostics. You keep the car powered; I monitor parameters and ensure no unexpected errors appear.

We test the functions together. I provide a summary, answer questions, and remain available for the 30-day warranty period.
